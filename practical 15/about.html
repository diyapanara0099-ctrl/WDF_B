About StudentHub

StudentHub is a basic student portal developed using HTML5 and CSS. It provides students with information about registration, events, profiles and academic activities.

Mission

Our mission is to provide students with an easy platform to access important college information.

Vision

Our vision is to improve communication between students and the institution through a simple online portal.

Objectives

  • Easy student registration
  • Display college events
  • Provide student information
  • Collect student feedback
